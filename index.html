Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Snake Lite ("we," "our," or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how data is handled while you play.

1. Information We Collect

Snake Lite is designed with offline-first gameplay. We do not require you to create an account or provide personal identifiers (such as your legal name, physical address, or phone number) to play.

  • Gameplay & Local Data: High scores, unlocked skins, coins, and local achievements are stored directly on your device storage.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and language settings may be processed automatically by integrated SDKs for performance optimization.
  • Crash & Performance Logs: Diagnostic logs to fix bugs and improve frame rates on lightweight devices.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To keep the game free to play, we display non-intrusive advertisements. These third parties may collect anonymized device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID) to serve relevant ads and measure performance:

  • Google AdMob: Serves banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads.
    Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services: Used for optional cloud saving, leaderboards, and achievements.
    How Google uses data

3. Offline Mode & Data Storage

When running in Offline Mode, no telemetry, gameplay stats, or advertising data is transmitted over the internet. When connected to Wi-Fi or cellular networks, standard ad mediation requests and leaderboard syncs resume automatically.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Snake Lite is a casual arcade title suitable for all audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently gathered data from a child, please contact us so we can promptly remove it.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

Because your game data is stored locally on your device, you can completely erase your progress, coin balance, and cached preferences at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App via your Android device settings.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or platform requirements. Any revisions will be updated directly on this page with a refreshed effective date.
